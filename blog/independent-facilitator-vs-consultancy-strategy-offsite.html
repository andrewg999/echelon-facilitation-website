Independent Facilitator or Large Consultancy for Your Strategy Offsite?

Dr Andrew Greenland • 3 October 2026 • 6 min read

Short answer: hire a large consultancy for your strategy offsite if you need new analysis, research or people to deliver a programme afterwards. Hire an independent facilitator if your leaders already have the information they need and the real problem is agreeing priorities, making trade-offs and sticking to them. Most leadership offsites fail for the second reason, not the first.

A strategy offsite is one of the most expensive days in a leadership team’s year. Once you add up salaries, travel, venue and the work that stops while everyone is away, the cost of getting it wrong is high. This guide compares the two most common ways of running one - with a large consulting firm or with an independent specialist facilitator - and explains how to decide.

The question to ask first: are you short of information or short of agreement?

Before comparing providers, be honest about what is holding your team back.

  • Short of information: you don’t know enough about your market, customers, costs or competitors to make a sound choice. You need research and analysis.
  • Short of agreement: your leaders know the issues well, but they disagree about priorities, avoid naming what has to stop, or agree in the room and then drift apart. You need facilitation.

Large consultancies are built to solve the first problem. Independent facilitators are built to solve the second. Many teams buy the first when they actually have the second.

When a large consultancy is the right choice

  • You are entering a new market, making an acquisition or restructuring, and need detailed research before anyone can decide.
  • You need people to deliver a large programme after the offsite.
  • Your board or investors expect an external, branded view of the strategy.

In these cases, the offsite is a step in a larger piece of work, and the consultancy’s analysis is what you are paying for.

When an independent facilitator is the right choice

  • You have held strategy sessions before that ended in slide decks rather than decisions.
  • Priorities compete and nobody has named what will be stopped or slowed.
  • One or two voices tend to dominate, and others stay quiet until after the meeting.
  • There is tension between leaders that gets in the way of decisions.
  • You want the strategy to belong to the team, not to an outside firm.

A specialist facilitator’s job is not to have the answer. It is to design a session where your team reaches its own answer, writes it down and commits to it in front of each other.

What a well-run strategy offsite produces

Whoever you hire, judge them on what you will have in writing at the end. For a leadership team, that should include:

  • A decision log: every decision, the options considered, the trade-off made, a named owner and a deadline
  • A one-page priority map: the top priorities, ranked and resourced, with what has been de-scoped
  • A stopped list: work the team has agreed to stop or slow, ready to communicate
  • A review plan: when and how commitments will be checked over the following weeks

If a provider can’t tell you what documents you will leave with, the offsite is likely to end in discussion rather than decisions.

Questions to ask before you hire anyone

  1. Who will actually design and run the session on the day?
  2. What will we do before the offsite, and what will you ask our leaders to prepare?
  3. How will you stop the most senior person from anchoring everyone else?
  4. What happens when two leaders disagree strongly?
  5. What will we have in writing at the end, and what happens 30 days later?

Frequently Asked Questions

Should we use a facilitator or a consultant for our strategy offsite?

Use a consultant if you need new research or analysis before you can decide. Use a facilitator if your leaders already have the information and the problem is agreeing priorities, making trade-offs and following through.

What does an external facilitator do at a leadership offsite?

An external facilitator designs the session around the decisions your team needs to make, runs it neutrally, manages disagreement and status dynamics, and makes sure every decision leaves the room with an owner and a deadline.

How much does a strategy offsite facilitator cost in the UK?

General workshop facilitation often costs around £1,200 to £2,500 a day, while executive and board-level facilitation typically costs £3,000 to £6,500 a day or more. Echelon’s 1.5-2 day Strategy & Vision Offsite costs £7,500 to £10,000, including preparation and follow-up.

Why do strategy offsites fail?

Most fail because they end in discussion rather than decisions: priorities are listed but not ranked, nobody names what will stop, and no one checks commitments afterwards. Good design, a neutral facilitator and a written decision log prevent this.

Can a facilitator work alongside our consultants?

Yes. A common approach is for consultants to provide analysis before the offsite and for an independent facilitator to run the session in which the leadership team makes its decisions.
