The Echelon Decision Log Template

The same decision log template we use in high-stakes leadership sessions. Designed to capture decisions, owners, and commitments - not discussion points.

A good decision entry includes: what was decided (not ‘discussed’), what was given up, who owns it, and how you will know it worked.

Each decision entry captures:

  • Decision statement - clear and specific, not ‘should we...’ but ‘we will / we will not...’
  • Options considered - 2-4 options that were genuinely evaluated
  • Trade-offs accepted - what you’re giving up or de-prioritising
  • Owner with name, role, and deadline
  • Dependencies, risks, and mitigations
  • Success criteria - how you’ll know the decision was right

Also includes:

  • Decision quality checklist (complete before ending the session)
  • Follow-up actions tracker linked to decisions
  • Guidance on writing strong vs weak decision statements

Writing Strong Decision Statements

Weak: ‘We discussed the marketing budget’ - this is a topic, not a decision

Weak: ‘We should look into hiring more sales staff’ - this is a suggestion, not a commitment

Strong: ‘We will increase Q2 marketing spend by £80k, funded by deferring the product relaunch to Q3. Owner: CMO. Deadline: 31 March.’
