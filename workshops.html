NEW AI Strategy

The AI Design Sprint

Best for: Leadership teams who know AI matters but can't agree on where to start, what to prioritise, or how to govern it responsibly

A structured 1-2 day sprint that takes your executive team from AI uncertainty to a prioritised, owned AI roadmap — without the hype, the vendor pitches, or the paralysis. You leave with concrete use cases ranked by impact and feasibility, a governance framework, and named owners for the first 90 days.

Why it works: Silent-first ideation prevents the loudest voice from anchoring on their preferred tool. Structured evaluation separates genuine business value from technology fascination. Every use case is scored against your actual strategic priorities.
  • Prioritised AI use-case portfolio ranked by business impact and feasibility
  • AI governance framework covering risk, ethics, data, and accountability
  • 90-day execution roadmap with named owners and decision checkpoints
  • Executive AI fluency — your team leaves able to evaluate AI proposals critically
🕑 1-2 days 👥 6-15 leaders

NEW Strategy

The Strategy Sprint

Best for: Leadership teams kicking off a new initiative, aligning on direction, or turning vision into a concrete, owned roadmap

A high-intensity structured workshop that takes your leadership team from an ambitious long-term goal through to a concrete roadmap with named owners — in one or two days. Six time-boxed exercises, silent-first ideation to prevent groupthink, and a designated Decider to ensure real choices get made.

Why it works: Silent-first exercises prevent anchoring bias. A designated Decider means decisions actually get made — not deferred. Every concept is written and self-explanatory. The roadmap uses broad time horizons that survive contact with reality.
  • Written long-term goal with measurable success metrics
  • Strategy concepts created, evaluated, and selected
  • Visual roadmap with named owners and 30-day action plan
  • Full Decision Log documenting every choice and rationale
🕑 1-2 days 👥 5-12 leaders

Decision Rights

The Decision-Rights Reset

Best for: Teams where decisions are slow, political, or get unmade after the meeting

A focused half-day session that maps the decision landscape, clarifies who decides what, and installs protocols that prevent the corridor reversal of agreed decisions.

Why it works: Makes invisible decision patterns visible. Creates shared rules everyone can hold each other to. Establishes escalation protocols before they are needed.
  • Mapped decision landscape: who decides what, at what level
  • Agreed decision protocols and escalation rules
  • Decision health score - baseline for improvement
🕑 Half day (3-4 hrs) 👥 5-10 leaders
