What You Get

What’s included, and what isn’t

Included in every price

  • A diagnostic call with the sponsor before anything is designed
  • Pre-work for every participant, so the session starts with their real views
  • A session designed around your decisions, not a standard agenda
  • Facilitation on the day by Dr Andrew Greenland
  • A decision log with owners, deadlines and trade-offs, plus a one-page summary
  • A 30-day follow-up call to check commitments are holding

Charged separately

  • Venue hire and catering
  • Travel and accommodation outside Greater London
  • A second facilitator for groups of more than 15

Virtual sessions carry no extra costs.

Founding Clients

Founding client rates for our first three engagements

Echelon is a new practice. Rather than invent case studies, we are offering our first three clients the founding prices shown above - around 30% below our standard rates.

In return, we ask for two things:

  • Permission to write up the session as an anonymised case study, which you approve before it is published
  • A short testimonial if you are happy with the outcome

The session itself is exactly the same as at standard rates. Once three founding places are taken, the offer closes.

The Real Comparison

What a day of your leadership team already costs

Take a leadership team of 12, each earning £150,000 to £200,000 a year. Spread across roughly 220 working days, one day of that team’s time costs around £8,000 to £11,000 in salary alone.

A strategy day that ends in more discussion spends that money and buys nothing. If the same decisions come back to the next meeting, you pay it again. The question isn’t whether facilitation costs money - it’s whether your team’s day produces decisions people act on.

FAQ

Pricing questions

How much does an executive workshop facilitator cost in the UK?

General workshop facilitation in the UK is often priced at around £1,200 to £2,500 a day. Executive and board-level facilitation, which involves more preparation and higher stakes, typically sits at £3,000 to £6,500 a day or more. Echelon’s full-day sessions are £4,500 to £6,000, including preparation and follow-up.

Why does a one-day session cost more than a day rate?

The day itself is only part of the work. Each price covers the diagnostic call, participant pre-work, designing the session around your decisions, writing up the decision log and a 30-day follow-up call.

Are there any extra costs?

Only venue and catering, travel outside Greater London, and a second facilitator for groups of more than 15. Virtual sessions have no extra costs.

What is a founding client rate?

Our first three clients pay around 30% less than standard rates in return for permission to publish an anonymised case study, approved by them, and a short testimonial if they are happy with the result.

How do I get an exact quote?

Book a 30-minute discovery call. We’ll talk through your team, the decisions you need to make and the right format, and send a fixed quote afterwards.
